The Story

My journey started in cloud infrastructure — automating server fleets at Hewlett Packard Enterprise, resolving production incidents, keeping systems alive at 3 AM.

Then AI changed everything. I moved to the US, earned my MS from the University of North Texas, and dove deep into agentic systems.

"An LLM will return a confident, well-formed, wrong number. My agents check the math."

Through the ABTalks 31 Days AI Cohort, I built production AI agents — invoice extractors that validate arithmetic, contract flaggers that never quote what they can't verify, and document intelligence tools that ship with verifiable outputs.
